About Rhys

Rhys Pritchard is a visual artist who is greatly affected emotionally by his environment.

He uses a variety of media in his work: water colour, oil paints, acrylics, oil pastels, and charcoal.

His work has been exhibited in the following shows:

  • "Northern Vision", a juried show at the Sudbury Art Gallery.
  • "Algoma Fall Festival Art Show", a juried show in Sault Ste. Marie at the Art Gallery of Algoma. Rhys's art won him 'Best in Show' and 'Best Watercolour in Show'.

Rhys can be contacted at: rhys.pritchard.art@gmail.com
